The mental model

Agents do the work. You make the decisions.

Six bounded specialists share one truthful workspace. They can research, extract, score, explain, and draft. They cannot silently turn a draft into a final application bundle: the Concierge asks before preparation and again before PDF rendering.

P

Profile Steward

Turns resumes, notes, project stories, reviews, education, interests, and preferences into reusable candidate facts.

S

Sourcing Scout

Refreshes configured public ATS and RSS sources, normalizes postings, tracks versions, and keeps discovery recent.

M

Match Analyst

Separates hard eligibility from weighted fit signals, then shows covered skills, visible gaps, and supporting evidence.

A

Application Coach

Keeps applications, follow-ups, contacts, notes, statuses, and their audit trail organized.

D

Document Tailor

Writes and designs job-specific resumes and cover letters, flags risky claims, and renders approved versions through Gotenberg.

F

Forth Concierge

Understands your request, routes it to the right specialist, reports results, and holds every approval in one place.

✓

Your source evidence remains the authority. Generated material is a draft until you review and approve it. Never approve a claim you could not comfortably explain in an interview.

First session

Start with one useful result

  1. Create your account with an email and password. If this beta requires an invitation code, enter the code shared with you.
  2. Choose Find jobs for me or Help me apply to a job.
  3. Upload your resume, paste its text, or choose Start without a resume. You can explore jobs without one; add your real experience before preparing application documents.
  4. Confirm target roles, location and work preferences. Work authorization is optional when you are unsure; missing details remain unconfirmed.
  5. Choose Find my first matches to start discovery, or continue to add a public job link or full description. If a link cannot be read, paste the posting text.
  6. Use Save & finish later to keep unfinished preferences in your account. Daily searches are optional and can be paused.
  7. Review a match, prepare documents, confirm any flagged claims, and apply on the employer’s site. Track the result in Applications.

The detailed profile interview is optional: open My Profile → Review my experience when you want to improve your evidence. Quick setup does not verify claims for you.

From a new role to final PDFs

Candidate Profile

Job workspace

Open Documents in the same workspace. Inspect the resume and cover letter, check flagged claims, and mark each current version as reviewed. Generation alone does not mean reviewed.

Job workspace

Applications

Apply on the employer site

Review draft answers in Employer form, open the employer link, upload documents and submit there. Opening the link does not mark the job as applied.

Employer website

Record submission and follow up

Choose Mark as applied and confirm the date. If you already applied using your own documents, use I already applied. Add notes, contacts and reminders in Tracking.

Applications

Candidate intelligence

Make your profile broad, specific, and true.

1

Search brief

Capture the choices that affect eligibility: desired roles, industries, current location, authorized countries, work modes, compensation, headline, and professional summary.

2

Preferences

Use must for hard requirements, strong for meaningful preferences, flexible for tradeable choices, and avoid for active negatives.

3

Profile facts

Facts are the reusable evidence layer. Verify accurate extractions. Edit vague titles or statements. Delete duplicates and anything you cannot substantiate.

4

Source material

Add resumes, project notes, work samples in text form, performance reviews, education, career stories, and relevant interests. Resume onboarding supports PDF, DOC/DOCX, HTML, ODT, RTF, Markdown, and text.

!

After changing your search brief or facts, recompute a job from Opportunities if you want its existing score refreshed immediately. Future imports and source runs use the latest profile automatically.

Discovery

Find jobs without configuring sources.

Open Job search settings and choose Find jobs for me. Forth uses your saved roles, location and work preferences to search public employer postings through Brave Search.

Start a search

Your first search uses sensible defaults. Repeated clicks reuse a search already in progress.

Keep your preferences

Edit your roles, location and work modes anytime. Forth does not silently loosen them to fill the results.

Daily discoveries

Opt into daily searches during setup, or use Advanced search settings to choose a different cadence.

Pause anytime

Use Pause scheduled searches to stop automatic discovery. Saved jobs remain available.

Bring your own posting

In Jobs, add a public employer link or paste a complete description. This works even when discovery is unavailable.

Understand an empty search

Review the search outcome and rejected pages. Edit preferences deliberately, or add a posting yourself.

Search progress and history are saved. You can leave the page and return while discovery continues. Advanced settings let you refine queries and thresholds when needed.

Decision support

Read the score, then read the evidence.

Forth embeds your complete candidate profile and every normalized job profile with OpenAI, then uses pgvector cosine search to bring the closest opportunities and supporting profile facts forward. Structured requirements and hard constraints still remain visible instead of being hidden inside the AI score.

Opportunity detail

Eligibility is shown separately from fit.

82

Hard eligibility

Pass, review, or uncertain reflects constraints such as location, authorization, and work mode. Treat uncertainty as a prompt to verify the posting.

Six fit signals

Skills, evidence quality, whole-profile semantic fit, role direction, domain relevance, and logistics are weighted separately so the total is explainable.

Confidence

Confidence describes the evidence quality behind the score. A high score with low confidence still deserves manual inspection.

Gaps and evidence

Visible gaps show missing or weakly supported requirements. Evidence links the positive case back to candidate facts.

Conversation + control

Delegate in plain language.

Ask what to focus on, request a source refresh, ask why a role scored the way it did, or prepare a specific opportunity. The Concierge routes the request and posts the specialist’s response into the conversation.

Useful prompts

  • “What should I focus on today?”
  • “Where is my profile weakest?”
  • “Why is this role a good match?”
  • “Prepare my strongest opportunity.”

Decisions waiting

Agent-requested preparation and final rendering pause here. Preparing directly from a job workspace starts immediately. Read the request, then choose Approve or Not now. Rejecting a request cancels that run without creating hidden side effects.

Evidence-backed materials

Review drafts as carefully as you would write them.

Canonical resume→Job requirements+Verified facts→Tailored drafts→Your approval

Prepare documents in the job workspace. The Documents tab contains both drafts, version history, resume design controls, evidence checks and PDF downloads. Use More actions to generate new versions. The separate Document library lets you browse historical versions and manage source resumes and contact details.

Marking a document as reviewed records your decision for that version; it does not submit an application. New versions need a fresh review. You can download drafts or use your own documents when applying.

Application operations

Keep the next action attached to the application.

Select a card to open its job workspace. Saving a job begins tracking; it does not mean you have applied. Record a submission explicitly, with or without generated documents. Status changes appear in the history. Follow-up reminders are separate from application stages.

Final outputs

Artifacts are versioned and private.

Open Documents in the job workspace to review and download a resume or cover letter as PDF. Previously exported files remain available under that heading. Files are fetched through your authenticated session; direct public media links are disabled.

✓

Before sending a file, check the employer name, role title, contact details, page breaks, bullet wrapping, and whether every claim still feels natural in your own voice.

Long-running work

You should never have to guess whether it is working.

Buttons change label, show a spinner, and disable duplicate clicks while their request is active. Durable background work also appears in a fixed activity card, so you can move to another screen without losing status.

StatusMeaningWhat you should do
QueuedThe worker has accepted the task.Keep using Forth; no need to click again.
Processing / RunningExtraction, sourcing, scoring, or an agent step is active.Wait for the completion message. You may navigate elsewhere.
Waiting approvalA specialist reached a human decision boundary.Open Concierge and review the request.
Ready / SucceededThe result is stored and available.Review the new facts, roles, drafts, or artifacts.
FailedThe attempt ended without completing.Read the message, correct configuration or input, and retry once.

When something looks wrong

Fast checks before retrying.

A source finishes with an error

Read the search outcome, retry if a service was temporarily unavailable, or paste a posting in Jobs. Check Advanced search settings only if you changed them. A missing discovery service must be configured by the operator.

A score looks unexpectedly low

Check hard eligibility, confidence, missing requirements, and supporting facts. Add or verify relevant evidence, correct the search brief, then choose Recompute on the opportunity.

I cannot approve a draft

Review the unsupported-claims list. If the claim is wrong, do not approve the version. If it is accurate and you can substantiate it, check the explicit risk-review box and approve.

Documents still say Needs review

Mark both the current resume and the current cover letter as reviewed. Reviewing an older version does not review a new draft. If only a resume exists, generate new document versions to create both documents.

The live-updates indicator is offline

Your normal page actions still use the API, but background updates may not arrive immediately. Refresh once after the task has had time to finish. If it remains offline, verify that Redis, the web service, and the reverse proxy are running.

PDF rendering falls back to HTML

The content is preserved, but Gotenberg was unavailable or timed out. Check the Gotenberg service, then request a new final bundle after it is healthy.
