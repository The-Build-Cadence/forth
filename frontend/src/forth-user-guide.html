The mental model

Agents do the work. You make the decisions.

Six bounded specialists share one truthful workspace. They can research, extract, score, explain, and draft. They cannot silently turn a draft into a final application bundle: the Concierge asks before preparation and again before PDF rendering.

P

Profile Steward

Turns resumes, notes, project stories, reviews, education, interests, and preferences into reusable candidate facts.

S

Sourcing Scout

Refreshes configured public ATS and RSS sources, normalizes postings, tracks versions, and keeps discovery recent.

M

Match Analyst

Separates hard eligibility from weighted fit signals, then shows covered skills, visible gaps, and supporting evidence.

A

Application Coach

Keeps applications, follow-ups, contacts, notes, statuses, and their audit trail organized.

D

Document Tailor

Writes and designs job-specific resumes and cover letters, flags risky claims, and renders approved versions through Gotenberg.

F

Forth Concierge

Understands your request, routes it to the right specialist, reports results, and holds every approval in one place.

✓

Your source evidence remains the authority. Generated material is a draft until you review and approve it. Never approve a claim you could not comfortably explain in an interview.

First session

Your 10-minute setup

  1. Choose Create your workspace, enter your email and a secure password, and continue. Forth signs you in immediately.
  2. Give the Profile Steward your current resume. PDF, Word, HTML, ODT, RTF, Markdown, and text files are supported; scanned PDFs use OCR when needed.
  3. Answer the adaptive follow-ups. The Steward reassesses the whole profile after each turn, proposes an evidence-based draft you can accept or edit, skips facts the resume already makes clear, and asks you to confirm anything genuinely ambiguous. Every submitted turn is saved and survives closing, refreshing, or signing back in.
  4. Complete the search brief: target roles, location, authorization, work modes, industries, and minimum compensation.
  5. Review Profile facts. Verify accurate facts, edit imprecise ones, and delete anything untrue.
  6. Add your must-haves, strong preferences, flexible choices, and avoidances.
  7. Open Opportunities and paste one job description to see your first evidence-backed score.

That is enough context to use scoring and tailoring. Add project notes, performance reviews, education, and hobbies later when they add meaningful evidence or clarify the kind of work you want.

Applications

Candidate intelligence

Make your profile broad, specific, and true.

1

Search brief

Capture the choices that affect eligibility: desired roles, industries, current location, authorized countries, work modes, compensation, headline, and professional summary.

2

Preferences

Use must for hard requirements, strong for meaningful preferences, flexible for tradeable choices, and avoid for active negatives.

3

Profile facts

Facts are the reusable evidence layer. Verify accurate extractions. Edit vague titles or statements. Delete duplicates and anything you cannot substantiate.

4

Source material

Add resumes, project notes, work samples in text form, performance reviews, education, career stories, and relevant interests. Resume onboarding supports PDF, DOC/DOCX, HTML, ODT, RTF, Markdown, and text.

!

After changing your search brief or facts, recompute a job from Opportunities if you want its existing score refreshed immediately. Future imports and source runs use the latest profile automatically.

Discovery

Use compliant, explicit sources.

Manual imports require no configuration. Live sources use the free Jobicy and Arbeitnow feeds, public Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby boards, or RSS endpoints. Give the source a clear name, select its kind, and provide JSON configuration.

Greenhouse

{"connector":"greenhouse","board_token":"acme","company":"Acme"}

Lever

{"connector":"lever","site":"acme","company":"Acme"}

Ashby

{"connector":"ashby","board":"acme","company":"Acme"}

Jobicy

{"connector":"jobicy","count":30,"geo":"canada","industry":"engineering","tag":"platform engineer"}

Arbeitnow

{"connector":"arbeitnow","remote_only":true,"max_results":30,"keywords":["engineering","software"]}

RSS

{"connector":"rss","url":"https://example.com/jobs.xml","company":"Acme"}

Choose Run to refresh a source. A persistent activity card lets you leave the page while discovery, normalization, and scoring continue. Demo-tagged sample sources are illustrative and safely return no live postings until you replace their configuration.

Decision support

Read the score, then read the evidence.

Forth embeds your complete candidate profile and every normalized job profile with OpenAI, then uses pgvector cosine search to bring the closest opportunities and supporting profile facts forward. Structured requirements and hard constraints still remain visible instead of being hidden inside the AI score.

Opportunity detail

Eligibility is shown separately from fit.

82

Hard eligibility

Pass, review, or uncertain reflects constraints such as location, authorization, and work mode. Treat uncertainty as a prompt to verify the posting.

Six fit signals

Skills, evidence quality, whole-profile semantic fit, role direction, domain relevance, and logistics are weighted separately so the total is explainable.

Confidence

Confidence describes the evidence quality behind the score. A high score with low confidence still deserves manual inspection.

Gaps and evidence

Visible gaps show missing or weakly supported requirements. Evidence links the positive case back to candidate facts.

Conversation + control

Ask what to focus on, request a source refresh, ask why a role scored the way it did, or prepare a specific opportunity. The Concierge routes the request and posts the specialist’s response into the conversation.

Evidence-backed materials

Review drafts as carefully as you would write them.

Canonical resume→Job requirements+Verified facts→Tailored drafts→Your approval

The studio is generation-first: choose a target opportunity and Forth writes, structures, and designs the complete resume from your verified evidence. Review the faithful page preview, design rationale, keyword coverage, unsupported claims, and risk notes; regenerate if needed, then approve and download the PDF. There is no manual formatting canvas.

Approving does not submit an application. It freezes your review decision for that exact version so the pipeline can request final rendering.

Application operations

Keep the next action attached to the application.

Select a card to update its status, follow-up date, contact, and notes. Status changes create activity events. Forth prevents an application from being marked applied when its selected resume is still a draft.

Final outputs

Artifacts are versioned and private.

Each rendered resume and cover letter is stored as an artifact connected to the application and exact source version. Open the application workspace and choose Download beside the file under Application materials to fetch it through your authenticated session. Direct public media links are disabled.

✓

Before sending a file, check the employer name, role title, contact details, page breaks, bullet wrapping, and whether every claim still feels natural in your own voice.

Long-running work

You should never have to guess whether it is working.

Buttons change label, show a spinner, and disable duplicate clicks while their request is active. Durable background work also appears in a fixed activity card, so you can move to another screen without losing status.

When something looks wrong

Fast checks before retrying.

A source finishes with an error

Validate the JSON first. Confirm the connector name and its required key: optional filters for Jobicy and Arbeitnow, board_token for Greenhouse, site for Lever, board for Ashby, or a public URL for RSS. Private and loopback URLs are rejected by design.

A score looks unexpectedly low

Check hard eligibility, confidence, missing requirements, and supporting facts. Add or verify relevant evidence, correct the search brief, then choose Recompute on the opportunity.

I cannot approve a draft

Review the unsupported-claims list. If the claim is wrong, do not approve the version. If it is accurate and you can substantiate it, check the explicit risk-review box and approve.

Render PDF bundle is rejected

The current resume and, when present, cover letter must both be approved. Return to Document Studio, approve the exact drafts, then request rendering again.

The live-updates indicator is offline

Your normal page actions still use the API, but background updates may not arrive immediately. Refresh once after the task has had time to finish. If it remains offline, verify that Redis, the web service, and the reverse proxy are running.

PDF rendering falls back to HTML

The content is preserved, but Gotenberg was unavailable or timed out. Check the Gotenberg service, then request a new final bundle after it is healthy.
